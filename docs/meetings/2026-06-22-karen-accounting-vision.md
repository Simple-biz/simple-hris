## What Karen Wants

### Currency & Paystubs

- **USD-primary currency display.** All dollar amounts should be shown in **USD**, with **PHP in
  subscript** where applicable. The biggest violator of this today is the **accounting dashboard**.
- **US values on paystubs.** Paystubs should list US (USD) values — including for Filipino
  employees.

### Language & Naming

- **Rename "Disputes."** Karen doesn't like the word "Disputes." Come up with a nicer name —
  "Issues" came up as a candidate.
- **Consistent name formatting.** Karen likes last names, so keep them, but standardize on the
  `Last, First "Nickname"` format. Several people break this trend (Aceli is an example) — clean
  that up.

### New "People" Tab (Accounting + CEO/Carla)

A searchable list of **all employees** with the following abilities:

- View **hours worked this week** and **pay rate**.
- View **banking information**.
- **Individually send a pay disbursement** and mark that the disbursement was done in the database
  of past payrolls — e.g., alongside the list of all of Darrell's payroll, a "special transfer" of
  X PHP on Y date. This process should be **very straightforward**.
- **Overtime monitoring** — how many hours each person is on track for.

### Payroll Wizard Streamlining

- **Don't shut down most of the system during payroll.** Accounting should retain full access to
  almost everything. Produce a short list of what is locked down and make it as short as possible.
- **Freeze past accounting cycles** + add a **note** capability. No information on past cycles
  should change, but we should be able to attach a note — e.g., *"We were wrong to deny Darrell's
  attendance bonus; this is adjusted on the paycheck dated 7/3/27."*
- **Rephrase the "Dispatch" screen.** Make it clearer what happens when the "Dispatch" button is
  pressed, and add **at least one additional approval step** so payroll isn't sent out early by
  accident.
- **Validation step needs delay/skip.** Ability to delay or skip certain **people** and certain
  **departments**. Example: exclude the PM team while Scott is out of town, then a few days later
  send payroll to just the PM team once Scott is back.
- **Contractor stage cleanup.** When status is "approved" or "rejected," the right-side quick
  approve/reject button is confusing (two contradicting buttons). Remove the right-most button
  (next to "Reset") and keep only **"Reset"**, which returns the invoice to "pending" for approval.

### Access Control & Security

- **Master account.** A root-access account, above all other accounts (including Kane's), with a
  login only **Cana and Rob** know — so Kane can be quickly removed if needed.
- **Hide pay rate from Managers & HR.** Pay rate should be exclusive to **Accounting & CEO**.
- **MESA approval.** MESA numbers should require **manager approval** if non-negative.

### Banking

- **Employees can't choose their bank.** We will **assign** them a bank; they should not be allowed
  to choose one.

### Visual / Brand

- **Color scheme consistency.** In a few places the colors are off. Ensure all "blue" is the
  **Simple.Biz blue**, and that logos are cropped correctly — especially in headers.

### Process / Documentation

- **Procedures document.** Build a procedures document on how to handle all functionalities of the
  new system. Avoid what happened with Fran — make sure multiple people (including Karen, especially
  for payroll disbursement) are familiar with the process and can step into anyone's role when
  needed.
